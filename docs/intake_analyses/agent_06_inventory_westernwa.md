## Mining in Western Washington (Hodges 1897)

### West-of-Cascades Commodity & Geology

**Au-Ag-Cu porphyry system**:
- Porphyry + porphyritic granite + quartz + hornblende + metamorphic modifications (host rocks)
- **Free gold at surface** (oxidized zone) → **base-metal sulfides at depth** (chalcopyrite, bornite, pyrite)
- Silver invariably present (few oz to significant); copper content 6–50%+ (native copper masses to 1000 lbs recorded)
- **Characteristic problem**: Surface mills (stamp mills, free-milling) succeeded; depth ore required concentration (concentrators, tables) — new technology, unfamiliar to 1890s operators

### Investment Cycle (1897–1910)

**Boom phase (1897–1900)**:
- Chief Moses' Reservation opening; massive staking
- Salmon River, Palmer Mountain, Cascade Range discoveries
- Free gold at surface attracted investors expecting indefinite shallow ore body

**Bust phase (1900–1910)**:
- Surface ore depleted or ore base-metallized (deeper levels)
- Stamp mills without concentrators failed; operators unfamiliar with base-metal reduction
- Silver price crash (1890s) bankrupted low-grade Salmon River operations
- Expert skepticism: "low-grade ore... broken formation... impossible to follow ore body to depth"
- Result: Investor confidence collapsed; most operations abandoned

**Exception**: **Monte Cristo** — successful depth development, modern mills, sustained faith + capital

### Districts & Status (1897)

| District | Commodity | Character | Fate |
|----------|-----------|-----------|------|
| Monte Cristo | Au-Ag-Cu | Successful depth development | Sustained production |
| Palmer Mountain | Au (surface) → Cu-Ag (depth) | Free gold attractive | Exploratory; depth potential uncertain |
| Salmon River | Ag-Cu (low-grade) | Investment bait → bust | Suspended (silver price crash) |
| Silver Creek | Au-Ag | Early discovery | Exploration phase |
| Index | Au-Ag-Cu | Investor interest | Limited production |
| Skagit Copper Belt | Cu (native + chalcopyrite) | Accessible ore bodies | Early-stage |
| Snoqualmie Basin | Au (placer); Cu (misidentified as iron) | Exploration | Scattered attempts |

**Key insight**: West-of-crest mining failed not due to lack of ore, but due to **metallurgical mismatch** (free-milling vs. base-metal concentration) + **lack of regional expertise** + **investor psychology** (speculation > development discipline)
